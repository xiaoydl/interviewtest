# Leave policy

Everyone gets twenty days of annual leave per year, accrued monthly.

Unused leave carries over for three months and then expires. Carry-over above five
days needs approval from your manager.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Absences under three days do not require a
medical certificate. Longer absences require one within five working days.

Parental leave is available after twelve months of continuous service: sixteen weeks
at full pay for the primary carer, six weeks for the secondary carer.

Leave requests go through the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer
than three days.
