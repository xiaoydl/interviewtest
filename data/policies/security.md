# Security policy

Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Check this before joining a network.

A lost or stolen device must be reported within one hour, to the security team and to
your manager. After one hour the incident is treated as a data breach.

Production access is read-only by default. Write access requires a second approver and
expires after seven days.

Customer data must never be copied into a personal account, a spreadsheet on a personal
device, or a third-party tool that has not been through security review.
