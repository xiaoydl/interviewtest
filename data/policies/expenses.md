# Expenses policy

Claims must be submitted within thirty days of the expense being incurred. Claims older
than sixty days need director approval and are paid in the next cycle.

Receipts are required for anything above twenty dollars. Below that, a line in the
monthly card statement is enough.

Travel: economy for flights under six hours. Premium economy for anything longer, and
business class needs prior approval from a director.

Client meals are capped at sixty dollars per person. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Software subscriptions need a named owner and a renewal date recorded in the asset
register.
